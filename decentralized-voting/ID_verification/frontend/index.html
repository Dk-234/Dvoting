📝 How It Works

  1. Connect your wallet — Link your MetaMask wallet (your verified identity will be tied to this address)
  2. Enter your details — Fill in your ID type, ID number, name, and date of birth exactly as on your ID
  3. Upload your ID photo — Take a clear photo of your government-issued ID
  4. Cross-verification — The system extracts text from the ID image using OCR and compares it with the details you entered
  5. Face verification — Take a live selfie video for liveness detection and facial verification
  6. Register your device — Bind your identity to this device using WebAuthn (biometric/PIN)
  7. Blockchain registration — Register a combined multi-factor proof hash on the blockchain
  8. Get authorized — Proposal creators can see your verified status and authorize you to vote
⚠️ Your actual ID number is NEVER stored. Only a secure hash is saved on the blockchain.
⚠️ Your face data stays on YOUR device — only a hash is sent to the backend.
⚠️ Each ID can only be registered with ONE wallet address to prevent duplicate voting.
